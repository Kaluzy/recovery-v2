High-protein meal ideas
Simple rules

Protein first. Build the meal around a protein source.

Vegetables next. Use them for volume and fiber.

Carbs measured. Keep a useful portion rather than stacking several large starches.

Added fat measured. Nuts, oil, mayo and dressing are useful but calorie-dense.

On Orthodox fasting days use tofu, lentils, split peas, chickpeas and other plant foods instead of animal products.
